# The Ring of Gyges, Chapter 1

J's Blender project (`ch1.blend`, saved with Blender 5.3): a 2.5D cut-out animation in a neon black-figure style.
Socrates, Aria and Cleon as Greek vase figures with neon outlines; a city set (protest crowd, banners) and a temple set;
medallion vignettes (Shadows & Fire, Wolf & Sheep, Pooled Coins, Ship, Scroll, Bridge) and Pathos → Logos callouts.
Eevee, 1920×1080, 12 fps, frames 88–6052. The file has no sound; J's voice tracks are in `tracks/` (one per character, 511 s, all starting at frame 0: frame = 12 × seconds, which the file's own keys confirm, e.g. Socrates's spotlight comes on at frame 744 = 62.0 s as his first line starts).

Rendered by the render server with job `gyges-ch1` (source "blend"). Chapter 2 is `film/gyges` (The Ring of Gyges II).

Chapter 1 with its voices (sent to J on 2026-11-14; not stored here, because LFS uploads from the agent's sandbox were refused) is made from frames 0–87 (a still opening the file skips, under the Narrator's first line) rendered here and joined to the render server's frames 88–6052, with the four tracks mixed to −16 LUFS. 504 s.
