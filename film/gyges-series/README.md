# The Ring of Gyges II, with the series' voices

The same script, sets and direction as `film/gyges`, re-voiced with the voices of J's *The Ring of Gyges* series (the tracks in `film/gyges-ch1` … `gyges-ch6`). Those turned out to be stock Kokoro voices, matched by pitch and timbre against all of Kokoro's English voices (each match well ahead of the next best; the method picks af_heart and bf_emma for Aria and the Narrator, which J had already chosen):

| Character | Voice |
|---|---|
| Socrates | Kokoro `bm_george` |
| Cleon | Kokoro `am_puck` |
| Julian | Kokoro `bm_lewis` |
| Aria | Kokoro `af_heart` |
| Narrator, News Anchor | Kokoro `bf_emma` |
| (Rex, Chapter 3) | Kokoro `am_fenrir` |

All at speed 1.0 (the series' pace measures the same), except the Narrator at 0.95 as before. Aria's and the Narrator's audio is unchanged from `film/gyges`; the men's 41 lines were spoken again, and every part re-directed from the new timing (`act*.scene.json`, `act*.film.json`). Render jobs: `jobs/gyges-ed-*` (Edward's style).
